Before You Come

Your links and options.

Everything below uses Microsoft's own pages. You can start free. I checked these details against Microsoft's pages in November 2026 and I'll check them again before each session, because Microsoft updates Copilot often. Its newest Copilot app came out in August 2026, and some settings moved.

Step by step

Getting set up

  1. Have a personal Microsoft account ready. If you use Outlook.com, Hotmail, Xbox, or sign in to Windows, you may already have one. Microsoft's guide shows how to check if your email is a Microsoft account.
  2. Open Copilot at copilot.com and sign in. Copilot is also available as an app for Windows, Mac, Android, and iPhone.
  3. If you have trouble signing in, Microsoft's sign-in help page walks you through it.
  4. If you have Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, or Premium, open Word or Excel and look for the Copilot button. We'll use it in Part 3.

Using a work or school account? Your organization controls what Copilot can do there, so please bring a personal account to follow along.

Free and paid

Plan options

  • Free: a limited free version of Copilot with a Microsoft account. It doesn't include Copilot inside Word, Excel, or Outlook.
  • Microsoft 365 Personal: $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year in the US, for one person. Adds Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and OneNote, with higher limits than free.
  • Microsoft 365 Family: $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year, for up to six people. The Copilot features are for the subscription owner only.
  • Microsoft 365 Premium: $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year, with the most Copilot use for most people.

You do not need a paid plan for Parts 1, 2, and 4. Microsoft offers a one-month free trial of Personal, Family, and Premium. See every plan on Microsoft's Copilot plans page.

Privacy

Settings worth knowing

  • In the newest Copilot app, your privacy choices are under Settings, then Personalization. That's where you can review or delete what Copilot remembers about you.
  • You can delete one chat, or your whole chat history, at any time.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in the same Personalization settings.
  • Read more in Microsoft's guide to your Copilot privacy controls and choices.
Inside the apps

Copilot in Word, Excel, and Outlook

  • Copilot in Excel works on files saved to OneDrive with AutoSave turned on.
  • You can turn Copilot off in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint if you'd rather not use it in a particular file or class.
  • Microsoft answers common questions in its Copilot in Microsoft 365 subscriptions FAQ.
