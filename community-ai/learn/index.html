Browse the directory

AI for Beginners runs live on Zoom as a four-part series. Pricing and the parts still open change as the series runs, so the course page is the only place that states them — check it for current dates, pricing, and registration.

What “free” means here. Each card says what the provider states. Some courses are free end to end; some are free to take with a paid exam or certificate; some are a free tier of a paid product. Where a provider has not stated the cost, the card says the cost is not confirmed rather than calling it free.

Some details are not on file. Course length, whether an account is required, and certificate details are not fields this directory collects yet, so no card claims them. Open the official page for those.

Verify before you enroll. Providers change their terms without notice. Every card links to its official page and shows when it was last checked — treat that source, not this page, as the authority.
