Group Training Program

Small Business AI Starter Program

A four-session program that helps small-business owners understand AI, save time on routine work, use AI responsibly, and leave with a realistic plan.

Request a quote. Pricing depends on group size, format, and materials.

Request a Program Proposal Ask a Question

Who It's For

Designed for organizations to offer their members.

Chambers of commerce, business associations, small business centers, and economic development agencies can bring this program to the business owners they serve. I handle the teaching and materials. You bring the participants.

No prior technical experience is required. If participants can send an email, they can take part. We start with the basics and build from there.

The Four Sessions

One clear step at a time.

1. Understanding AI for Everyday Business

What AI is, what it can and can't do, and how to choose tools that fit your business and budget.

2. Using AI to Save Time

Drafting communications, organizing information, planning, and handling routine business tasks, with hands-on practice.

3. Using AI Responsibly

Checking accuracy, protecting private information, and understanding where AI falls short.

4. Building an AI Action Plan

Each participant picks one useful workflow and prepares a realistic plan to put it into practice.

What's Included

Materials participants keep.

  • Participant workbooks to follow along and take notes.
  • Guided exercises using real, everyday business tasks.
  • An AI safety checklist for deciding what information to keep out of AI tools.
  • A follow-up consultation for the sponsoring organization after the program.
