About This List

A curated list, not a directory dump.

Checked against the official source

Every activity is confirmed against the organization’s own website, a municipal page, or a state or federal page before it appears here. Aggregators and social posts can point to something worth adding, but they are not what publishes it.

Blank rather than guessed

Hours, pricing, ages, accessibility and reservation rules change constantly, so this list does not repeat them. Where a detail is not confirmed, the field is empty. Unknown is better than wrong.

Nothing publishes itself

An activity reaches this page only after it has been reviewed and marked for publication. Verifying a source and publishing an entry are two separate decisions, and both have to happen.

The companion to Village Notes

Village Notes covers Connecticut care, support and community resources. This covers what to do for the fun of it. Same search, same filters, same standards — different question.
