Darice on AI

Smarter, more responsible AI. Made easier.

I share practical tips for using AI well, written for everyone, whatever your role or experience level. I skip the hype and the jargon and stick to clear help you can put to use right away.

Join the weekly newsletter →

What you'll get

One short, useful issue each week.

I keep every issue plain and honest about what AI does well and where it falls short.

  • 01Practical tips and prompts you can try the same day.
  • 02Plain explanations of new AI tools and features, without the marketing spin.
  • 03Guidance on using AI responsibly, including privacy, accuracy, and where a human still needs to decide.
  • 04Content for every level, from your first time trying AI to leading it across a team.

You don't need a technical background to follow along, whether you're just getting started or already use AI every day.

About

Who writes this.

I'm Darice Corey, a senior IT and AI strategy leader. I spend my days helping people use new technology in ways that are practical and responsible, and I kept finding myself writing the same tips for people who wanted to use AI but didn't know where to start. This newsletter grew out of that. My goal is to help you use AI more confidently and more wisely.
