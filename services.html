Consultations

Cancellation and refund policy.

Consultations are $350 and booked through my Zoom scheduling page, where you pay and pick a time in one step.

Canceling or rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule using the link in your confirmation email, or by emailing me at info@daricecorey.com.

48 hours or more before your appointment

You'll receive a full refund, or you can move your session to another available time at no charge.

Less than 48 hours before your appointment

The consultation fee is not refundable. You may reschedule once, depending on my availability.

Missed appointments

If you don't join within 15 minutes of the start time, the session is considered missed and is not refundable.

If I need to cancel

You'll receive a full refund or the option to rebook at a time that works for you.

Emergencies

If something unexpected comes up, reach out and I'll do my best to work with you.

How refunds work

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take several business days to appear.
