AI Consulting & Training, Darice Corey, LLC

Practical AI Solutions for Small Businesses, Nonprofits, and Organizations

I help small businesses, nonprofits, and organizations understand where AI can make a difference, identify tools that fit their needs and budgets, and introduce AI into everyday work responsibly.

My approach is practical. I focus on helping people understand what AI can do, where human judgment is still needed, and how to get started without unnecessary complexity or expense.

What I Can Help You Solve

Common questions I hear.

  • "Where do we even start?" I help you pick one or two tasks where AI can actually help, instead of trying everything at once.
  • "Which tools should we pay for?" I compare options, including free and low-cost ones, against what you need.
  • "Is it safe to put our information in there?" I help you decide what should never go into a public AI tool and how to check what comes out.
  • "Our staff are all over the place." I help you set shared expectations so people know what is OK, what needs review, and who to ask.
  • "We want training our people will actually use." My sessions are hands-on, and everyone leaves with something they can try the next day.

Why Work With Me

Practical experience, explained in plain language.

I started in IT in a college computer lab, and much of what I know I taught myself on the job. That shapes how I teach. I don't assume anyone already knows the vocabulary, and I make room for questions.

My professional background includes 19 years in higher education technology, where I currently serve as Senior Director of Web and IT Planning at Yale College. I have taught college information technology courses since 2003. I hold an MBA, and I am an Executive DBA candidate at Fairfield University, where my research interests center on AI governance. That research is still in progress, so I share what I am learning as questions and practice, not as finished findings.

Through Darice Corey, LLC, I work independently. My Yale experience is part of my background. It does not mean Yale is involved in, sponsors, or endorses my business. Read the Business Independence Statement.
