Ask Darice: Website Builder

Privacy for the Website Builder, in plain language.

Ask Darice: Website Builder is a connector from Darice Corey, LLC that works inside Claude. It walks small business owners through planning a website and putting it online. This page explains what the builder sees, what it keeps, and how to have it removed. I don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

What's collected

What the builder sees and keeps.

  • 01 Your Google sign-in. When you connect the builder, you sign in with Google. Google shares your name, your email address, and your Google account ID with the builder. The builder never sees your Google password. I use these details only to let you in, to check that your email is on the invite list during the pilot, and to know which websites are yours.
  • 02 Your connection. To keep you signed in, the builder stores a connection record with your Google account ID, name, and email in secure storage on Cloudflare, the service that runs the builder.
  • 03 Which websites are yours. When you publish a website, the builder saves a short record that links the website's address to your Google account ID. That is how it makes sure only you can update your site or take it offline.
  • 04 Your answers and your website. The builder saves the answers you give in each step, and a description of your website, in secure storage on Cloudflare under your account. That way you can pick up where you left off, and update your website later from any chat without starting over. Your answers are deleted automatically 12 months after you last change them. The description of a published website is kept while it is online and deleted when you take it offline. You can ask Claude to delete your saved answers at any time. Your conversation with Claude is handled by Anthropic under its own privacy policy.
  • 05 Your photos. If you add photos, the builder shrinks each one and removes hidden details, such as where it was taken, before saving it. Your photos are kept in secure storage on Cloudflare under your account for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically. You can remove any photo at any time. When you publish, your photos become part of your public website. Only add photos you took or have permission to use.
  • 06 Your published website. When you say yes to publishing, the builder puts your website online through Cloudflare. Anything on your published website, including any phone number or email you chose to show, is public on the internet. You can take your website offline at any time by asking the builder, and its files are removed.

Protections

What the builder will not take.

The builder is built to refuse passwords, card numbers, bank numbers, Social Security numbers, tax ID numbers, and verification codes. If you type one by mistake, it will tell you to enter it only on the vendor's own website.

Tools you choose to add to your website.

If you add a contact form (Formspree), a chat helper (Wonderchat), or visitor statistics (Google Analytics) to your website, those services handle your visitors' information under their own policies. Your website includes its own short privacy section that names the tools you turned on.

Who can see your information.

Only you and I can see the records described above. As the administrator, I can take a website offline if it breaks the law or puts people at risk, or if you ask me to. I don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Services the builder relies on

Third-party policies.

  • •Google for sign-in (Google Privacy Policy)
  • •Cloudflare to run the builder and host published websites (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)
  • •Anthropic for Claude, where your conversation happens (Anthropic Privacy Policy)

The builder's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Your choices

How to remove your information.

Email info@daricecorey.com from the address you signed in with and ask me to remove your information. Within 30 days, I will take your websites offline if you want, delete your photos, saved answers, and connection and ownership records, and confirm by email. You can also disconnect the builder in Claude's connector settings at any time.

Who the builder is for.

The builder is for adults running a business or organization. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes and questions.

If this page changes, I will update the date below. While the pilot is small, I will also email pilot users about any major change. Questions? Email info@daricecorey.com.
