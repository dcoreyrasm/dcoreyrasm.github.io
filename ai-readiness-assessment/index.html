AI Readiness Assessment

Who It's For

Built for organizations just beginning to look at AI.

This assessment is a good fit for small-business owners, solo entrepreneurs, nonprofits, and organizations that are curious about AI but aren't sure where it fits. You don't need any technical background, and you don't need to have used AI tools before.

The goal is simple: help you understand which tasks might benefit from AI, what to be careful about, and what to do first, so you can make good decisions without spending money on tools you don't need.

What's Included

What you can expect.

  • Initial consultation. We talk through your goals, your team, and the work that takes the most time.
  • Review of up to three business workflows. We look closely at how the work gets done today.
  • Practical AI opportunities. I identify where AI could realistically save time or improve quality.
  • Privacy and responsible-use review. I flag information that should stay out of AI tools and where human review is needed.
  • Suggested tools and low-cost alternatives. Options that fit your budget, including free ones.
  • Written AI readiness summary. A short, plain-language report you can share with your team or board.
  • Prioritized 30-day action plan. Clear first steps, in order, with who does what.

Deliverables are confirmed in a written proposal before work begins.

How It Works

From first call to action plan.

  1. Inquiry and proposal. You tell me about your organization, and I send a written scope and price.
  2. Consultation and workflow review. We meet virtually to walk through up to three workflows.
  3. Summary and action plan. I send your written readiness summary and 30-day plan.
  4. Follow-up conversation. We review the plan together and answer remaining questions.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to share private data?
No. I review how work gets done, not your customer records. If an example would help, we use made-up or redacted information.
Is this legal or compliance advice?
No. I help you think through responsible use and practical risks. For legal, regulatory, cybersecurity, or compliance questions, I'll recommend you check with a qualified professional.
What happens after the assessment?
Many organizations take the action plan and run with it. If you'd like more help, the Starter Program or Adoption & Governance Consulting can build on it.
