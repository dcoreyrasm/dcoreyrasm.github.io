Who It's For

For people who want to start now, without a big budget.

This course is for new and future business owners who have an idea, a small budget, and a reason to get started right away. You don't need a tech background.

I started Darice Corey, LLC in September 2026 and used AI to build my brand, my website, my newsletter, and my payment links. In this course, I'll show you how, step by step, so you can do the same for your own business.

I teach in plain language, show my own real setup as the example, and then show you other options you can choose instead. I've spent more than 35 years in IT and over 20 years teaching it at the college level, and I built my own business this fall using these exact steps.

We start with free tools first. When a paid tool is actually worth it, I'll tell you.
