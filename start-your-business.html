Who It's For

People starting on a small budget.

This course is for people who want to start a business now, without a big budget for designers, developers, and consultants. You don't need a tech background. You need an idea, a computer, and two hours on twelve Sunday afternoons.

I teach with the tools I actually used, and I'll show you other options for each one. I'm also honest about cost. You can start with free plans, and I'll tell you when a paid plan is worth it. A few sessions, like updating your website with Claude Code, work best on a paid Claude plan.

I've spent more than 35 years working in IT and teaching it at the college level, and I'm currently an executive doctoral student researching AI governance. I teach in plain language, with real examples and plenty of room for questions.

How It Works

Getting your Zoom link.

  1. Choose the full course or a single session above and complete checkout.
  2. After you pay, register for the Zoom session using the same email address you used to pay. The confirmation screen tells you how.
  3. I'll match your Zoom registration to your payment and approve it. Zoom will then email you your personal join link.
  4. You'll also get an email receipt from Stripe. If you don't see your Zoom email, check your spam folder or email me and I'll help.

Each join link is personal to the registered participant and works only for that session, so please don't share it.

Sessions are recorded. The recording shows my screen and slides, not your face or name. This course doesn't give legal or tax advice. For those questions, please talk with an attorney or an accountant.
