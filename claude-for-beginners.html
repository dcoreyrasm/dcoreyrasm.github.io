About the Series

Who it's for.

This series is for people who keep hearing about AI and want a calm, plain-language place to start. Maybe you've tried ChatGPT and want to see how Claude compares, or maybe you've never used an AI tool at all. Either way, you're welcome here.

Each part builds on the one before it, and each one also works on its own, so you can join for just the parts you want. Bring something real you'd like help with.

I've spent more than 35 years working in IT and more than 22 years teaching it at the college level, and I'm currently an executive doctoral student researching AI governance. I teach in plain language, with real examples and plenty of room for questions.

How It Works

Getting your Zoom link.

  1. Choose a single part or the full series above and complete checkout.
  2. After you pay, you'll land on a thank-you page with a link to register for the Zoom session. Please register using the same email address you used to pay. Each part has its own Zoom registration.
  3. I'll match your Zoom registration to your payment and approve it. Zoom will then email you your personal join link.
  4. You'll also get an email receipt from Stripe. If you don't see your Zoom email, check your spam folder or email me and I'll help.

Each join link is personal to the registered participant and works only for that session, so please don't share it.
