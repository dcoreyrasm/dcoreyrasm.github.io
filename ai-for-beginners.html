About the Session

What you'll learn.

  • How to create a free ChatGPT account and find your way around
  • Which privacy settings to check before you start
  • A simple way to ask for what you need
  • How to tell when an answer needs a second look
  • What it means when AI tools can take actions on your computer

The session is a 30-minute guided workshop, followed by open practice and Q&A. Bring something real you'd like help with, like an email you've been putting off or a meal plan for the week.

This is Part 1 of my four-part AI for Beginners series. I've spent more than 35 years working in IT and teaching it at the college level, and I'm currently an executive doctoral student researching AI governance. I teach in plain language, with real examples and plenty of room for questions.

The Series

What we'll cover in all four parts.

Each part builds on the one before it, so we go a little deeper into ChatGPT every time, and every part shows you how to use it safely. You don't need to be an expert by Part 2.
