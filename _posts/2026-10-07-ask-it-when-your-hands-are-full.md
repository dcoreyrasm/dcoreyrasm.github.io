Some questions come up at the worst time, like when you’re stirring a pot, carrying groceries, or driving. This week is four ways to ask your AI without typing. Two work from a car dashboard, so keep your eyes on the road and treat anything you hear as a first draft. AI can sound sure and still be wrong, so check anything about money, health, or safety against a real source, and keep private details out of any tool you don’t fully trust.

## Claude: ask Claude with Siri or the Share menu (iPhone)

![Claude: Ask Claude with Siri or the Share menu (iPhone)](/assets/blog/2026-10-07/claude.png){: .section-image }

On an iPhone, you can hand Claude a question without opening the app first.

1. Make sure your iPhone is on iOS 18 or later and the Claude app is installed.
2. Say “Ask Claude” to Siri, or type “Ask Claude” into Spotlight search.
3. In any app, you can also select text, tap Share, and choose Ask Claude.
4. For a request you repeat, open the Shortcuts app, add the Ask Claude action, type your prompt, and name the shortcut.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it when you’re mid-task and don’t want to open an app. Anthropic’s page says it needs iOS 18 or later, uses whichever model is the default in your Claude app, and counts toward your usage limit. That page covers iPhone shortcuts, not the car.

Source: [Claude Help Center: Use Claude app intents, shortcuts, and widgets on iOS](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10263469-use-claude-app-intents-shortcuts-and-widgets-on-ios)

## ChatGPT: chatGPT in CarPlay

![ChatGPT: ChatGPT in CarPlay](/assets/blog/2026-10-07/chatgpt.png){: .section-image }

Start a voice chat from your car’s screen, or pick up an earlier chat, without touching your phone.

1. Update the ChatGPT app and check that your iPhone is on iOS 26.4 or later.
2. Connect your iPhone to your car’s CarPlay and select ChatGPT.
3. Choose “New voice chat” and wait for the screen to change from “Connecting” to “Listening.”
4. Speak your question. To continue an earlier conversation, open a pinned or recent chat from the same screen.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it to talk something through, like planning a weekend or rehearsing what you want to say. OpenAI lists it for every ChatGPT plan. It can’t see maps, your car, or your live location, and it can’t control other apps like Maps or Messages, so keep using your regular navigation. OpenAI’s own guidance is to stay hands-free while driving.

Source: [OpenAI Help Center: Using ChatGPT in CarPlay](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001153-using-chatgpt-in-carplay)

## Copilot: microsoft Copilot in Apple CarPlay

![Copilot: Microsoft Copilot in Apple CarPlay](/assets/blog/2026-10-07/copilot.png){: .section-image }

Microsoft added CarPlay support to its Copilot app for iPhone, listed in its September 23, 2026 release notes.

1. Install the Microsoft Copilot app on your iPhone.
2. Connect your iPhone to Apple CarPlay in your car.
3. On the CarPlay screen, select Microsoft Copilot.
4. Say “Hey Siri, ask Copilot” to start a chat, then keep going by voice.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it to look something up or plan a task while you drive, which is how Microsoft describes it. The release note calls it the Microsoft Copilot app on iOS and doesn’t spell out plan or region details, so check the app before you count on it. As of this writing, that is all I can confirm.

Source: [Microsoft Learn: Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes (September 23, 2026)](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/release-notes)

## Gemini: gemini in your car (Android Auto)

![Gemini: Gemini in your car (Android Auto)](/assets/blog/2026-10-07/gemini.png){: .section-image }

On an Android phone with Android Auto, or a car with Google built in, you can talk to Gemini from the dashboard.

1. For Android Auto, you need an Android 11 or later phone with at least 2GB of RAM, and Gemini set as your default assistant.
2. Connect your phone to the car.
3. Say “Hey Google” (Voice Match needs to be on), tap the microphone on the car’s screen, or press the voice button on your steering wheel.
4. Ask for something like “find a supercharger near a sushi restaurant that’s open now,” or say “Let’s talk Live” for a back-and-forth chat. Say “Stop” to end it.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it for quick errands or a chat on a long drive. Google says plainly that Gemini in Android Auto is new, can get facts wrong, and shouldn’t be relied on for critical or safety-related matters while you drive. It isn’t in every language or region, and anyone in the car can use it, including kids.

Source: [Gemini Apps Help: Chat with Gemini in your car](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16735982)

## Try this

Pick one thing your hands are usually too busy for, like a recipe question while you cook or planning tomorrow on a commute. Try whichever of these matches your phone and ask, “Help me plan tomorrow in three steps.” Then check any real detail, like a time or an address, against a real source before you act on it. If you’re driving, stay hands-free and keep your eyes on the road.
