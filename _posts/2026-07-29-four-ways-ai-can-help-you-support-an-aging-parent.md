If you are helping look after a parent, most of the mental load is just remembering things: medications, appointments, forms, follow-ups. All four tools now have a way to dig into something once or keep checking on it for you, instead of you carrying it all in your head. A few of these need a paid plan, noted below, but you can still ask the same question by hand for free either way. One serious caveat: never treat an AI answer as medical or legal advice. Always confirm anything about health, money, or benefits with a real doctor, pharmacist, or advisor.

## Claude: run several searches at once with Research

![Claude: run several searches at once with Research](/assets/blog/2026-07-29/claude.png){: .section-image }

Claude's Research mode runs several searches on its own and pulls them into one answer with sources, instead of you searching around yourself.

1. Click the plus sign in the lower left of your Claude chat.
2. Select Research from the menu. A blue indicator confirms it is on.
3. Ask your question, like "compare in-home care agencies near [city] and what Medicare actually covers."
4. Claude runs multiple searches and gives you one answer with sources you can check yourself.

**When to reach for it:** before a big decision, like choosing between assisted living options or understanding what a new diagnosis actually means. This needs a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise); without it, you can still ask Claude the same question, just without the multi-search research mode.

Source: [Claude Help Center: Use research on Claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11088861-use-research-on-claude)

## ChatGPT: put reminders on a schedule with Tasks

![ChatGPT: put reminders on a schedule with Tasks](/assets/blog/2026-07-29/chatgpt.png){: .section-image }

ChatGPT can check on something or remind you about it on a schedule, so you are not the only one keeping track.

1. Open the Scheduled section in the ChatGPT sidebar, or just ask directly, like "remind me every evening to check that Mom took her medication."
2. Set how often it should happen and roughly what time.
3. Confirm it. ChatGPT shows you a card with the scheduled details.
4. Adjust how you get notified under Settings, then Notifications.

**When to reach for it:** for the recurring stuff that is easy to forget: a daily medication check, a weekly call reminder, a monthly bill due date. This needs a paid plan (Plus, Pro, Business, or Enterprise), and tasks cannot run more than once an hour.

Source: [OpenAI Help Center: Scheduled Tasks in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10291617-tasks-in-chatgpt)

## Copilot: hand off a recurring job with Copilot Tasks

![Copilot: hand off a recurring job with Copilot Tasks](/assets/blog/2026-07-29/copilot.png){: .section-image }

Copilot Tasks lets you describe a goal in plain language and have Copilot handle it, on its own or on a schedule, instead of walking it through step by step.

1. Open Copilot and go to the Tasks view.
2. Describe what you want done, like "email me a weekly summary of Dad's upcoming appointments."
3. Choose to run it now or set it to repeat.
4. Check in on the results as they come in. Copilot shows you what it did before treating anything as final.

**When to reach for it:** for something you would otherwise have to remember to do yourself every week. This feature is in preview and tied to a paid Copilot tier rather than the free version, so check what your plan includes before counting on it.

Source: [Microsoft Support: Using Copilot Tasks](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/using-copilot-tasks)

## Gemini: set a recurring reminder with Scheduled actions

![Gemini: set a recurring reminder with Scheduled actions](/assets/blog/2026-07-29/gemini.png){: .section-image }

Gemini can run a prompt on a schedule you set, daily, weekly, or monthly, and let you know when it is ready.

1. Go to gemini.google.com.
2. Type your request with the schedule built in, like "every Monday morning, remind me to refill Mom's pill organizer."
3. Submit it. Gemini confirms the scheduled action.
4. Manage it anytime under Settings and help, then Scheduled actions, where you can pause, edit, or delete it.

**When to reach for it:** for the small recurring things that fall through the cracks, a weekly refill reminder, a monthly check on a benefits deadline. This needs a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription (or a qualifying Workspace account), and you can run up to 10 at once.

Source: [Gemini Apps Help: Schedule actions in Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16316416)
