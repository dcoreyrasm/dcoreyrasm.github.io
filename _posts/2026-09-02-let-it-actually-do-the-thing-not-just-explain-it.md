Most of the time, AI gives you an answer and you still do the legwork: click through the settings, fill in the form, compare the options tab by tab. All four tools below can now go a step further and actually work through a real task while you do something else. Just read the plan before you approve it, especially anything touching money, your accounts, or your computer, because a confident plan can still be wrong.

## Claude: cowork

![Claude: Cowork](/assets/blog/2026-09-02/claude.png){: .section-image }

Cowork is Claude's mode for handing off a whole multi-step project instead of one question at a time, so it can work through the steps in the background while you do something else.

1. Open Claude on the web, the desktop app, or mobile, and choose Cowork instead of Chat in the message box.
2. Describe the outcome you want, not just a question, for example “organize my downloads folder by project and rename the files.”
3. Review the approach Claude lays out before it starts.
4. Step away. Claude works through the task on its own and comes back with the finished result, whether that's organized files, a document, or a written summary.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it when a task has real steps to it, not just an answer to look up, and you'd rather review finished work than babysit the process. It needs a paid plan (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise), and any task that touches your local files, browser, or computer needs the Claude desktop app open and connected.

Source: [Anthropic Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)

## ChatGPT: agent mode

![ChatGPT: Agent mode](/assets/blog/2026-09-02/chatgpt.png){: .section-image }

Agent mode lets ChatGPT use its own browser to actually complete a task, not just describe how you'd do it yourself.

1. Open a chat and choose Agent mode from the tools menu, or type /agent.
2. Describe the task, for example “find weekend flights to Denver next month and put the three cheapest in a table.”
3. Look over the plan ChatGPT lays out before it starts.
4. Let it work. It pauses to check with you before anything that needs your say-so, like entering payment details.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it for a task with real steps in a browser, not a quick question. It needs a paid plan (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Edu), and each plan caps how many agent tasks you get a month, from 40 on Plus up to 400 on Pro.

Source: [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11752874-chatgpt-agent)

## Copilot: agent in Settings

![Copilot: Agent in Settings](/assets/blog/2026-09-02/copilot.png){: .section-image }

Agent in Settings lets you describe a change you want on your PC in plain language, then approve it so Copilot makes the change for you instead of just pointing you to the right menu.

1. Open Settings on a Windows 11 PC and look for the agent option in the left-hand navigation or the new Settings home page.
2. Type the change you want in your own words, for example “make my screen easier to read” or “turn on dark mode.”
3. Review the fix the agent proposes.
4. Approve it, and the agent makes the change for you. Check the result and ask again if it's not quite right.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it for an actual setting change, not a how-to you'd have to follow yourself. Right now it needs a Windows 11 Copilot+ PC with a Snapdragon X Series processor, and it currently only understands English.

Source: [Microsoft, Windows Learning Center](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/learning-center/two-must-try-ai-tools-that-simplify-tasks)

## Gemini: auto browse in Chrome

![Gemini: Auto browse in Chrome](/assets/blog/2026-09-02/gemini.png){: .section-image }

Auto browse lets Gemini in Chrome work through a multi-step task on a website for you, like comparing options or starting a booking, while you use your phone for something else.

1. On Chrome for Android, tap Ask Gemini.
2. Describe the task, for example “compare the top three hotels near downtown Chicago this weekend and find the cheapest one with free cancellation.”
3. Review the plan Gemini proposes.
4. Tap Start Task and check back in. You can keep using your phone while it works, and pause or take over any time.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it for a real multi-step task on a site, not a quick lookup. It needs a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription, you have to be 18 or older and in the US, and for now it only works on Chrome for Android, not iPhone, iPad, or desktop.

Source: [Gemini Apps Help, Google](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16821166)

## Try this

Pick one small multi-step task you'd normally do by hand: reorganizing a cluttered folder, comparing a few options for something you need to buy, or fixing one setting you've been meaning to change. Describe the actual outcome you want to whichever tool you already use, not just a question, and see how far it gets before it needs you. Reading the plan before you approve it is still your job, especially if money or your accounts are involved.
