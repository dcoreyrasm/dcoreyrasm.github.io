I lose the thread constantly. I’ll spend twenty minutes comparing strollers or mapping out a weekend trip, close the tab, and two days later I’m starting over from nothing. All four of these tools now have some quiet way of holding your place, a recap, a pin, a browsing trail, so the plan you were building is still there when you open the app again. None of them will finish the plan for you, and I still wouldn’t hand any of them my confirmation numbers or account details. Keep anything that really matters somewhere you control, not just in a chat you’re trusting to remember it.

## Claude: monthly Recap

![Claude: Monthly Recap](/assets/blog/2026-08-30/claude.png){: .section-image }

Claude now shows you a recap of what you actually spent time on this month, a quiet way to notice the thread you meant to pick back up.

1. Turn on Memory in Claude’s settings, if it isn’t on already. The recap needs it to work.
2. Go to Settings, then Reflect.
3. Read your recap: the topics you spent time on, your most active day, and your peak hour.
4. Notice what you kept circling back to. That’s usually the thing worth finishing.

**When to reach for it:** once a month, when you want a nudge about what you were really working through, more like a highlight reel of your own conversations than a task list. It’s in beta on Free, Pro, and Max plans, web and desktop only, and it only shows up once Memory is turned on.

Source: [Claude Support: See your monthly recap](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15672559)

## ChatGPT: pin Chats and Projects

![ChatGPT: Pin Chats and Projects](/assets/blog/2026-08-30/chatgpt.png){: .section-image }

ChatGPT lets you pin an in-progress chat or project so it stays put in your sidebar instead of sliding down under everything newer.

1. Open ChatGPT on the web and find the sidebar.
2. Hover over the chat or project you want to keep close.
3. Click the menu next to it and choose Pin.
4. Check your Pinned section any time. Chats and projects both live there together now.

**When to reach for it:** for the plan you’re still building, a trip, a big purchase, a draft, that you don’t want to hunt for the next time you open ChatGPT. It won’t organize the rest of your history for you, so pin selectively.

Source: [OpenAI Help Center: ChatGPT Release Notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)

## Copilot: copilot Journeys (Microsoft Edge)

![Copilot: Copilot Journeys (Microsoft Edge)](/assets/blog/2026-08-30/copilot.png){: .section-image }

Copilot Journeys quietly groups your recent browsing into topic cards, so a multi-day research project is waiting for you instead of scattered across a dozen old tabs.

1. Open Microsoft Edge and go to Settings, then Copilot and AI.
2. Turn on Copilot new tab page, then turn on Journeys.
3. Keep browsing normally. Copilot groups related pages by topic as you go.
4. Open a new tab to see your Journeys and pick a topic back up, tabs and all.

**When to reach for it:** when you’re deep in comparison shopping or trip research across a pile of tabs and don’t want to lose your place. It’s free with a personal Microsoft account, but Edge only, and journey cards delete themselves after 14 days, so it’s a short memory, not an archive.

Source: [Microsoft Support: Copilot Journeys](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/copilot-journeys)

## Gemini: pin and Rename Chats

![Gemini: Pin and Rename Chats](/assets/blog/2026-08-30/gemini.png){: .section-image }

Gemini lets you pin the chat you keep coming back to and rename it something you’ll actually recognize later.

1. Go to gemini.google.com and open the side panel if it’s hidden.
2. Hover over the chat you want to keep handy, under Recent.
3. Click More, then Pin.
4. Rename it while you’re there. “Kitchen reno” beats “Chat 47” six weeks from now.

**When to reach for it:** for the one ongoing decision or project you keep returning to across several sessions, so you’re not scrolling back through weeks of chats to find it. It requires Keep Activity turned on for personal accounts, so check that setting first if pinning doesn’t stick.

Source: [Google Gemini Help: Manage your Gemini chats](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746)

## Try this

Pick one thing you’re actually in the middle of right now, a trip, a big purchase, a decision you haven’t made yet. Pin it, or turn on Journeys, in whichever tool you used to start it. In a week, see how fast you can find your way back in, versus how long it usually takes you to re-explain the whole thing from scratch.
