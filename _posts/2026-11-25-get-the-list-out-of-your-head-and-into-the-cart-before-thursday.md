Thanksgiving is tomorrow, and if you're like me, half the shopping list is still living in your head. This week I'm showing you four ways to get it out. Two of these tools can build a real grocery cart for you, and two can turn your notes or a quick description into a checklist. One honest note before you start: AI can miss an ingredient or double one just as confidently as it gets the rest right, so read the list against your recipes before you spend money, and keep payment details inside the store's own checkout.

## Claude: shop a Recipe With Instacart in Claude

![Claude: Shop a Recipe With Instacart in Claude](/assets/blog/2026-11-25/claude.png){: .section-image }

Connect Instacart and Claude can find stores near you, check what's in stock, and turn a recipe or meal idea into groceries in a cart.

1. Start a chat and paste your menu, for example: "Turkey, stuffing, green beans, and two pies for 10 people."
2. Ask Claude to put the ingredients in an Instacart cart. If it suggests the Instacart connector, add it with a click on desktop or a few taps on mobile.
3. Sign in with your Instacart account when it asks.
4. Go through the cart item by item and take out anything you already have. Claude is designed to check with you before it buys anything for you.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it when you want to go from recipe to cart in one conversation. Anthropic says connectors work on all plans, with mobile still in beta, and to only use connectors from developers you trust. You can disconnect it anytime.

Source: [Claude blog: New connectors in Claude for everyday life](https://claude.com/blog/connectors-for-everyday-life)

## ChatGPT: build and Check Out a Cart With the Instacart App

![ChatGPT: Build and Check Out a Cart With the Instacart App](/assets/blog/2026-11-25/chatgpt.png){: .section-image }

ChatGPT can put together an Instacart cart from local stores and let you check out without leaving the chat.

1. Ask something like "Help me shop for apple pie ingredients on Instacart." ChatGPT may also suggest the app when you talk about recipes or meals.
2. Connect the app and sign in with your Instacart account.
3. Look over the cart it builds from local retailers, and swap or remove anything you don't need.
4. When it looks right, check out in the Instacart app inside ChatGPT. Instacart finds a shopper to pick up and deliver the order.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it when you want the whole order, start to finish, in one place. OpenAI's announcement doesn't say which plans or regions have it, so check that it shows up for you. And look at the total before you pay, because this one spends real money.

Source: [OpenAI: Instacart and OpenAI partnership](https://openai.com/index/instacart-partnership)

## Copilot: turn Your Notes Into a List in OneNote

![Copilot: Turn Your Notes Into a List in OneNote](/assets/blog/2026-11-25/copilot.png){: .section-image }

Copilot in OneNote reads the notes on a page and pulls out a to-do list you can copy anywhere.

1. Open the OneNote page where your menu, recipes, or "don't forget" notes live.
2. On the Home ribbon, select Copilot to open the Copilot pane. You can also right-click the page and choose Copilot, then Create a task list.
3. Type what you want, like "Make a shopping list from the recipes on this page, grouped by store section," and select Send.
4. Refine it with another instruction, or select Copy to paste the list wherever you shop from.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it when the list is already buried in notes you wrote. It works in OneNote for Microsoft 365, and Microsoft says that if you don't see Copilot there, it may not be part of your subscription or your organization may have it turned off. It only works from what's on the page, so it won't catch the ingredient you never wrote down.

Source: [Microsoft Support: Create a to-do list and tasks in OneNote with Copilot](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/create-a-to-do-list-and-tasks-in-onenote-with-copilot-95fdfbeb-d499-4024-9387-5416ab56a58e)

## Gemini: help Me Create a List in Google Keep

![Gemini: Help Me Create a List in Google Keep](/assets/blog/2026-11-25/gemini.png){: .section-image }

Describe what you need and Gemini drafts a checklist right inside Google Keep.

1. Open the Google Keep app and tap Create.
2. At the bottom right, tap Help me create a list.
3. Enter a specific prompt, like "Thanksgiving groceries for 8 people, one vegetarian, one gluten-free," and tap Create.
4. If it's off, edit your prompt and tap Refresh. When it's close, tap Insert to save it as a list.

**When to reach for it:** reach for it when you want a fast starting list on your phone. Google says it needs a Google AI plan, mentions Pixel devices, may run into Gemini usage limits, and warns that suggestions can be inaccurate. Treat it as a first draft, not a recipe check.

Source: [Google Keep Help: Create a list with Gemini in Google Keep](https://support.google.com/keep/answer/15222789?hl=en)
