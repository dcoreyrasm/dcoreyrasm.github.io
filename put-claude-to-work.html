About the Course

Who it's for.

This course is for people who already have a business or side project and already use Claude for everyday tasks. If you've taken my Claude for Beginners series, this is the next step. If you're newer to Claude, Claude for Beginners is the better place to start.

Each part builds on the one before it, so the full course gets you the most. Bring your real business with you. We'll work on your tasks, your apps, and your voice, not made-up examples.

Some of what I show uses a paid Claude plan. I'll point out which parts need one as we go, so you can decide what's worth paying for.

I've spent more than 35 years working in IT and more than 22 years teaching it at the college level, and I'm currently an executive doctoral student researching AI governance. I teach in plain language, with real examples and plenty of room for questions.

  • Leaving with one connected workflow and one scheduled task

Safety focus: what AI can see and do once it's connected to your accounts or allowed to act on your computer.

How It Works

Getting your Zoom link.

  1. Choose the full course or a single part above and complete checkout.
  2. After you pay, you'll land on a thank-you page with a link to register for the Zoom session. Please register using the same email address you used to pay. Each part has its own Zoom registration.
  3. I'll match your Zoom registration to your payment and approve it. Zoom will then email you your personal join link.
  4. You'll also get an email receipt from Stripe. If you don't see your Zoom email, check your spam folder or email me and I'll help.

Each join link is personal to the registered participant and works only for that session, so please don't share it.
