Brave & Bright: Girls in STEM

A Coloring Book for Curious Minds

This coloring book has 29 designs of girls exploring science, technology, engineering, and math, with encouraging messages to build curiosity and confidence in STEM. I made it to invite young minds to ask questions, try new things, and imagine what they can create.

Along the way, girls will find messages like these:

  • I belong in STEM.
  • My questions matter.
  • I can build amazing things.

About the Author

Why I made this book.

I’ve worked in technology for more than 35 years, and I’ve taught college IT courses since 2003. I created this book to help girls see themselves in STEM, and to give them an early, friendly reminder that they belong there.
