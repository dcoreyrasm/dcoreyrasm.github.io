title: "Plan a Short Demo Video for a Resource"
category: Presenting and Sharing
description: "Turn a tool, template, or guide you made into a timed voiceover script and scene plan for a short demo video."
published_on: 2026-11-06
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  I made a resource I want to share in a short recorded demo video. Please help me plan it.

  What it is: [describe your tool, template, checklist, or guide in two or three sentences]
  Who will watch: [your audience, such as peers at a conference or a team meeting]
  Length: [target length, such as 5 minutes]
  The video needs to cover: what the resource is, why I created it, and how I put it in place.
  Look and feel: [your colors, fonts, or "keep it simple and clean"]

  Leave out names, account numbers, and anything private. If you need a detail I haven't given you, ask me instead of guessing.

  Please give me:
  1. A voiceover script in my voice, in first person, plain and warm, with no jargon. Split it into short scenes, and keep it within my target length at a natural speaking pace.
  2. For each scene, a short plan for what appears on screen: a headline, a few words of supporting text, and which item should appear on which line of the script.
  3. One scene that walks a single example through the resource from start to finish, and label it as an illustration.
  4. A closing scene with an invitation to reach out with questions.
  5. A short list of anything in the script that I should double-check before I record.
how_to:
  - "Fill in the brackets with a plain description of your resource and audience."
  - "Read the script out loud once and change any line that doesn't sound like you."
  - "Build the scenes in your slide or video tool, record your voice, and add captions so everyone can follow along."
watch_out: "Don't paste in screenshots or files that show real names, private data, or internal details. Describe your resource in your own words instead, and check every claim in the script before you record it."
