prompt: |
  I'm thinking about applying for a fellowship or grant, and I want an honest assessment before I spend time on it.

  Here is the call for applications:
  [paste the full call, or at least the eligibility rules, focus areas, time commitment, and selection criteria]

  Here is a short summary of my background and current work:
  [a few sentences about your role, what you've built or led, and what you're working on now]

  Please do the following:
  1. Summarize the key terms: deadline, timeline, funding, time commitment, location requirements, and eligibility. If the call doesn't state something, say so instead of guessing.
  2. Tell me which track or focus area fits my work best, and why. If none fit well, say that plainly.
  3. Name the risks or gaps, such as a requirement I may not meet, a time commitment that could conflict with my job, or rules about ownership, licensing, or outside work I should check.
  4. Based on the selection criteria, tell me what the strongest applications will need to show.
  5. Ask me whether the pull here is a real career need or mainly the program's name, and help me answer that honestly.
  6. Suggest one or two pieces of my existing work I could feature as evidence.

  Keep what the call actually says separate from your own interpretation. Don't write my application answers for me, since many programs require applicants to use their own words. Leave out names, account numbers, and anything private.
how_to:
  - "Paste the full call for applications, or the sections that cover eligibility, time, and selection criteria."
  - "Add a few sentences about your background. Describe your work in general terms and leave out private details."
  - "Use the results to decide whether to apply, then write your answers in your own voice."
watch_out: "Check the program's own rules on AI use before you use AI for any part of an application, and confirm every date and requirement on the official page."
