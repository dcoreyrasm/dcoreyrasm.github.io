title: "Write your About page"
category: Small business
description: "Draft an About page for your website that sounds like a real person, not a brochure."
published_on: 2026-11-19
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  Help me write the About page for my business website.

  My business: [what you do and who you serve]
  Why I started it: [your reason, in a sentence or two]
  What makes my business different: [two or three things]
  How I want it to feel: [for example, warm, professional, or playful]

  Before you write, ask me three questions that would help my story stand out. Then write about 200 words in first person, in plain language, ending with one clear next step for the reader.
how_to:
  - "Fill in what you can. Short answers are fine."
  - "Answer the AI's questions with real details. Those make the page yours."
  - "Read it out loud and cut anything you wouldn't say to a customer in person."
watch_out: "Share only what you're comfortable having on a public website. Leave out your home address and family details."
