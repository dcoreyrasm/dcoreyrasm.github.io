prompt: |
  I own [business name], a [type of business] in [your town or area]. My customers are [who they are].

  Write five short social media posts for next week:
  1. A helpful tip
  2. A behind-the-scenes look
  3. An answer to a question customers often ask
  4. An offer or reason to visit
  5. A shout-out to my community

  Keep each post under 60 words, warm and plain. Before you write, ask me three questions about my business.
how_to:
  - "Fill in the brackets and paste the prompt into your AI tool."
  - "Answer its three questions. That's where your posts start to sound like you."
  - "Before you post, ask it to “rewrite these to sound like this” and paste in a post you wrote yourself."
watch_out: "Read every post before it goes out. Check any offer, price, or date yourself, because AI can get them wrong with confidence."
