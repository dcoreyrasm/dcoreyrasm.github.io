---
layout: prompt
title: "A calm reply to an upset customer"
category: Small business
description: "Turn a frustrated message into a clear, kind reply that solves the problem without making promises you can't keep."
published_on: 2026-11-19
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  A customer sent me an upset message. Here it is, with their name and details removed:

  [paste the message here]

  What happened from my side: [your short version of what happened]
  What I can offer: [refund, redo, discount, or a call, only what you're truly willing to do]

  Write a reply that acknowledges how they feel, explains what happened in one or two sentences without blaming them, and clearly says what I'll do next. Keep it under 120 words. Don't promise anything I didn't list.
how_to:
  - "Remove the customer's name and personal details before you paste their message."
  - "Be honest about what you can offer. The AI will only use what you list."
  - "Read the reply out loud before you send it. If it sounds stiff, ask for a warmer version."
watch_out: "If the situation involves a legal claim, an injury, or a threat, talk to a professional before you reply. AI isn't a lawyer."
