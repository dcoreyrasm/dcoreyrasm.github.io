title: "Describe a product or service"
category: Small business
description: "Write a short description that tells customers what they get and why it matters to them."
published_on: 2026-11-19
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  Write a description of one of my products or services for my website and social media.

  What it is: [name and short description]
  Who it's for: [your customer]
  What problem it solves or what it makes better: [in your words]
  Details customers need: [size, time, what's included, or other facts]

  Write three versions: one sentence for social media, a short paragraph for my website, and three bullet points. Focus on what the customer gets, not on fancy words. Use only the facts I gave you.
how_to:
  - "Fill in the details, including the facts customers always ask about."
  - "Pick the versions you like and adjust the wording."
  - "Check every fact and number before you post it."
watch_out: "If you sell food, health, or beauty products, don't let the AI add health claims. Only say what you know is true."
