---
layout: prompt
title: "Announce a price change"
category: Small business
description: "Tell customers about a price increase clearly and kindly, without over-apologizing."
published_on: 2026-11-19
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  I'm raising my prices for [product or service] starting [when]. The reason is [your honest reason, for example rising costs].

  Write a short message for my customers for [email, social media, or a sign]. It should:
  - Say clearly what's changing and when
  - Give the reason in one sentence
  - Thank them for their support
  - Mention anything that stays the same, or a way to lock in the current price: [optional]

  Keep it under 100 words, warm and confident. Don't over-apologize.
how_to:
  - "Fill in the change, the date, and your reason."
  - "Choose where you'll share it, and ask for a version for each place if you need more than one."
  - "Give customers some notice before the new price starts."
watch_out: "Double-check the dates and prices in the final message. A wrong number in a price notice is hard to take back."
---
