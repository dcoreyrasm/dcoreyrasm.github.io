title: "A customer reply kit"
category: Small business
description: "Build short, friendly replies to the questions you answer over and over, using only your real policies."
published_on: 2026-11-19
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  I run [business name]. Here are my real policies:
  - Hours: [your hours]
  - Prices: [your prices or price ranges]
  - Booking or ordering: [how it works]
  - Returns, refunds, or cancellations: [your policy]

  Write short, friendly replies to the five questions my customers ask most, plus a booking or order confirmation and a follow-up message for after the visit. Use only the policies I gave you. If something is missing, ask me instead of guessing.
how_to:
  - "Fill in your real policies. Don't let the AI make any of them up."
  - "Read each reply and change anything that doesn't sound like you."
  - "Save the final versions somewhere you can copy them from quickly, like your phone's notes."
watch_out: "Leave out customer names and contact details. If the AI adds a policy you didn't give it, delete it."
