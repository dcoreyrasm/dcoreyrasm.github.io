title: "Reply to an online review"
category: Small business
description: "Write a short, genuine reply to a happy or unhappy review that protects your reputation."
published_on: 2026-11-19
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  Help me reply to this online review of my business, [business name].

  Review: [paste the review here, with the reviewer's last name removed]

  Write a reply that:
  - Thanks them by first name only
  - Responds to what they actually said
  - Stays calm and kind, even if the review is unfair
  - If they had a problem, offers a way to make it right: [what you're willing to offer, or "invite them to contact us"]
  - Is under 80 words

  Give me two versions, one warmer and one more professional.
how_to:
  - "Paste the review and say what you're willing to offer, if anything."
  - "Pick the version that sounds most like you and adjust it."
  - "Wait until you feel calm before you reply to a negative review."
watch_out: "Don't share private details about the customer's order or visit in a public reply. Move those conversations to a private message."
