---
layout: prompt
title: "Follow up after a quote"
category: Small business
description: "Write a friendly nudge to a customer who hasn't answered your quote or estimate."
published_on: 2026-11-19
tools: [Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini]
fill_in: true
prompt: |
  I sent a quote to a potential customer [how long ago] for [what the job or order is]. They haven't answered.

  Write a short, friendly follow-up message for [email or text]. It should:
  - Remind them what the quote was for, without restating every detail
  - Make it easy to say yes, ask a question, or say not right now
  - Not sound pushy

  Then write a second, shorter follow-up I could send a week later if I still don't hear back.
how_to:
  - "Fill in the brackets and choose email or text."
  - "Add one personal detail from your conversation with them so it doesn't feel like a form letter."
  - "Put a reminder on your calendar for the second follow-up."
watch_out: "Leave out the customer's full name, address, and the exact price in the prompt. Add those yourself when you send it."
---
