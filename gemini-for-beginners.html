Before You Come

Your links and options.

Everything below uses Google's own pages. You can start free. I checked these details against Google's help pages in November 2026 and I'll check them again before each session, because Google changes plans and names often.

Step by step

Getting set up

  1. Have a personal Google account ready (a Gmail address works). If you don't have one, Google's Create a Google Account page walks you through it.
  2. Open Gemini at gemini.google.com and sign in. The Gemini app is also available for Android and iPhone.
  3. Open Gemini Notebook at notebook.google and sign in with the same account.
  4. Check your activity settings at myactivity.google.com/product/gemini. We'll walk through this together in Part 1.

Using a work or school account? Your organization's administrator controls some settings and features, so a personal account is the easiest way to follow along.

Free and paid

Plan options

  • Free: $0 with a Google account. Includes Gemini, Gems, Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas, Gemini Notebook, and 15 GB of storage.
  • Google AI Plus: a lower-cost paid plan with higher limits than free. Check Google's plan page for the current price.
  • Google AI Pro: $19.99 a month in the US. Higher usage limits, Gemini in Gmail, Docs, and more, higher Gemini Notebook limits, and 5 TB of storage. Paid plans are for ages 18 and up.
  • Google AI Ultra: the highest tier, for heavy users. You won't need it for this series.

You do not need a paid plan for this series. See every plan on Google's Gemini plans page.

Gemini Notebook limits

How much a notebook holds

  • Free: up to 100 notebooks, 50 sources in each
  • Google AI Plus: up to 200 notebooks, 100 sources in each
  • Google AI Pro: up to 500 notebooks, 300 sources in each

Google lists these as subject to change. See the current numbers on Google's Gemini Notebook plans page.

Privacy

Settings worth knowing

  • If you're 18 or older, the Keep Activity setting is on by default. It saves your Gemini chats to your Google account.
  • By default, activity older than 18 months is deleted automatically. You can change that to 3 or 36 months, or turn it off.
  • Even with Keep Activity off, Google keeps conversations for up to 72 hours to run the service.
  • Read more in the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub and Google's guide to managing your Gemini activity.
