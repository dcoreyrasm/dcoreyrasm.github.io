Private hair studio · Bridgeport, Connecticut

HWS by Danni: Private Salon Studio Website and Marketing

I partnered with Danielle, owner of HWS by Danni, a private hair studio in Bridgeport, CT, to rebuild how her business shows up online and runs behind the scenes. I built her website with an AI chatbot that answers visitor questions and points them to online booking. I also planned her brand refresh, service menu, and local search strategy, and set up client emails, care guides, and policies. I use AI tools to run much of the day-to-day work so she can stay focused on her clients.

What I built

  • The live website at hwsbydanni.com
  • An AI chatbot on the site that answers visitor questions and points them to online booking
  • A brand refresh and a tiered service menu
  • A local search plan for the studio and its on-location service area

What runs behind it

  • Client email setup for new and returning clients
  • Care guides that match each service
  • Clear client policies for booking, deposits, and visits
  • AI tools that handle much of the day-to-day work, so the owner can stay focused on clients

Speaker and author

Renée Bulwin, Bleed Into Purpose: Speaker and Author Website and Brand Kit

I built the website for Renée Bulwin, a speaker and author who founded Bleed Into Purpose, a platform for breaking the silence around childhood trauma. The site gives people a calm, gentle place to learn her story, find her book, book her to speak, and find support. I also put together a brand kit so a designer, printer, or event organizer can match her look and voice.

What I built

  • The live website at reneebulwin.com, on her own domain
  • Pages for her story, her speaking, her book, and support resources
  • A clear path to book a talk
  • Space ready for the workshops and Reiki sessions she is adding next

What runs behind it

  • A brand kit with her colors, type, logos, buttons, and photo guidance
  • A voice guide with the lines she approved, in her own words
  • A support note on wellness pages that points to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
Visit reneebulwin.com
