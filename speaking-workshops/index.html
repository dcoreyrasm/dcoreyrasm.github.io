AI Access & Workforce

No Laptop Required: Opening AI Access for the Learners Most Likely to Be Left Out

An interactive workshop focused on removing barriers to AI education and workforce opportunities. Using learner scenarios and printed AI examples, participants look at barriers like devices, cost, confidence, privacy, and time, then plan one practical change for their own program.

Intended audience

Adult educators, career and technical education and workforce professionals, nonprofit staff, counselors, and school administrators.

Available formats

60-minute interactive workshop. Unplugged: no slides, laptops, or AV required.

Participants will

  • Identify barriers to AI access, including connectivity, cost, confidence, trust, and time.
  • Evaluate AI-generated material for accuracy, bias, and privacy risks.
  • Create a 30-day AI access plan using free or low-cost resources and community partners.

AI Governance & Leadership

From Experimentation to Practice: How AI Governance Takes Shape in Organizations

A leadership session examining how responsible AI practices develop as organizations move from informal experimentation toward shared guidance. Participants work through practical scenarios involving privacy, review and approval, unequal access to tools, and responsible experimentation. The session draws on my ongoing research interests and does not present research in progress as established findings.

Intended audience

Higher education leaders, IT managers, nonprofit executives, professional associations, and governance groups.

Available formats

45 to 60-minute interactive workshop, or a 20-minute talk.

Participants will

  • Distinguish AI governance policies from everyday AI practices.
  • Identify common decision points for responsible AI experimentation and approval.
  • Draft a basic decision guide for AI use, review, and escalation.

Practical AI for Nonprofits

Practical AI for Nonprofits: Start Small, Protect Data, Make Time

A beginner-friendly workshop on using AI to support nonprofit work responsibly and affordably. Participants explore realistic uses for drafting communications, planning programs, and organizing information, compare free and affordable tools, and practice checking AI output.

Intended audience

Nonprofit executive directors, program teams, volunteers, community organizations, and small charities.

Available formats

60-minute interactive workshop, or a 30-minute introduction.

Participants will

  • Identify two or three low-risk nonprofit workflows where AI might help.
  • Use a basic checklist to check AI output for errors, privacy, and bias.
  • Design a 30-day AI pilot with a goal, an owner, and a review step.
